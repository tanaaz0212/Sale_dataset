# Sales Project Data Dashboard (Excel)

## Overview

The **Sales Project Data Dashboard** is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze sales performance across products, cities, categories, and customers. It provides a detailed view of how different factors contribute to overall sales.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze product-wise sales performance
* Compare sales across different cities
* Understand category contribution
* Identify top customers
* Evaluate payment methods

---

## Filters (Slicers)

* **Product:** Desk, Laptop, Mobile Phone, Tablet, etc.
* **City:** Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune
* **Category:** Accessories, Electronics, Furniture

---

## Key Metric

* **Grand Total Sales:** ₹582,530

---

## Dashboard Components

### 1. Category vs Sum

* Pie chart showing contribution of each category
* Electronics has the highest share

---

### 2. Count of Product by City

* Donut chart representing product distribution across cities

---

### 3. Sum of Unit Price by Customer

* Bar chart showing top customers
* Helps identify high-value customers

---

### 4. Total Sale by Product

* Line chart comparing product performance
* Highlights best and least performing products

---

### 5. Product Count (Payment Mode)

* Horizontal bar chart showing usage of payment methods:

  * UPI
  * Net Banking
  * Debit Card
  * Credit Card
  * Cash

---

## Insights

* Electronics category dominates sales
* Certain cities contribute more to total sales
* A small group of customers generates major revenue
* Digital payment methods are commonly used

---

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* Slicers

---

## Conclusion

This dashboard provides a detailed breakdown of sales data, helping users understand product performance, customer contribution, and city-wise distribution.
